    WHAT ARE ODTs?

    EXPERIENCE THE MISSION.

    Operational Development Training gives cadets the opportunity to experience the Air and Space Force beyond the classroom.

    Through visits, internships, leadership programs, and specialized training, cadets gain exposure to military careers, installations, missions, and professionals. These experiences help cadets better understand the opportunities available to them after commissioning.
